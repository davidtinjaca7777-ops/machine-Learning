Use Case Name

Credit Risk Assessment in Loan Approvals. The goal is to determine the probability of a customer defaulting before approving a loan.

Problem Context

Banks and financial institutions face significant losses when customers fail to meet their credit obligations. A precise risk assessment enables more informed decisions and protects the financial stability of the institution.

Data that could be used

Credit history, monthly income, debt level, age, employment history, type of loan requested and payment behavior.

Model Objective

The model should classify customers into risk categories (high, medium, low) or predict the probability of default. This helps in deciding whether to approve, reject or adjust the terms of the loan.

Type of Machine Learning

Supervised Classification algorithms like logistic regression, Random Forest, or neural networks can be applied. These models help identify patterns in historical customer data and predict future behaviors.

Potential Benefits

Reduction of default rates, increased profitability in the loan portfolio and better allocation of financial resources. Additionally, it enhances investor confidence and the stability of the banking system.
