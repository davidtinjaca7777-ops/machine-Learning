Conclusions

The choice of algorithm depends on the type of problem:

  • Logistic Regression: ideal for simple binary problems and high interpretability.
  • Random Forest: recommended for complex data and when robustness is required.
  • Linear Regression: useful for continuous predictions with linear relationships.
