What is Reinforcement Learning? An agent learns by trial and error to choose actions in an environment in order to maximize the accumulated reward. In this exercise, a virtual robot learns to travel from A to T on a 10×10 grid with walls # and danger zones D.

Q-Learning

Q(s,a) ← r + γ · maxa' Q(s',a')

Q is estimated with one SGDRegressor per action: predict() computes the Q-values and partial_fit() corrects them with the target r + γ·max Q(s',·) after every transition.
