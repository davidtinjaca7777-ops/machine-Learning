Case 3 – Customer Segmentation

  • Model Objective

  • Benefits

Use Case Name

Customer Segmentation. The goal is to group customers into homogeneous categories based on their characteristics and behaviors.

Problem Context

Companies need to better understand their customers to design personalized marketing strategies. Without segmentation, there is a risk of offering irrelevant products or services and missing opportunities for customer loyalty.

Data that could be used

Demographic information (age, gender, location), purchase history, visit frequency, average spending, product preferences and digital behavior (clicks, website navigation).

Model Objective

The model should identify groups of customers with similar characteristics. This allows for the creation of segment profiles and the design of specific campaigns for each group.

Type of Machine Learning

Unsupervised Learning techniques such as K-Means or hierarchical clustering are applied. These algorithms allow for the discovery of hidden patterns without the need for prior labels.

Potential Benefits

Improvement in marketing campaign effectiveness, increased customer satisfaction, and optimization of resource allocation. Additionally, it enables the identification of high-value customers and the design of specific retention strategies.
