Problem Context

This application analyzes customer behavior to predict whether a person will make a purchase (1) or not (0). The dataset contains information such as age, monthly income, number of website visits, time spent on the site, previous purchases, and whether a discount was used.

  • Independent variables: Age, Monthly Income, Website Visits, Time on Site, Previous Purchases, Discount Used.
  • Target variable: Purchase Decision.
  • Classes: 0 = No Purchase, 1 = Purchase.
