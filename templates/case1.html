Use Case Name

Sales Forecasting in Retail. The goal is to anticipate product demand in physical and online stores.

Problem Context

Retail companies face fluctuations in demand due to seasonality, promotions, and consumption trends. Accurate forecasting enables efficient inventory planning and marketing strategies.

Data that could be used

Sales history, prices, promotions, customer count, store locations, external factors like weather or special events.

What the model should predict

The model should estimate future sales of each product based on historical and external variables. This helps anticipate demand and avoid stockouts.

Type of Machine Learning

Multivariate Linear Regression or more advanced supervised models like Random Forest or XGBoost. These algorithms allow capturing relationships between multiple variables and improving accuracy.

Potential Benefits

Inventory optimization, reduced storage costs, and increased customer satisfaction by avoiding stockouts. Additionally, it improves marketing campaign planning and strategic decision-making.
